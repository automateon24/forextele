Examples:
- NIFTY_expired_2025-02-01_2025-02-27_ATM_CALL_1min_MONTH_1.parquet
- BANKNIFTY_expired_2025-03-01_2025-03-27_ATM+1_PUT_1min_MONTH_1.parquet

- SENSEX_expired_2025-02-03_2025-03-05_ATM_CALL_1min_MONTH_1.parquet

Data Columns:
-------------
- timestamp: 1-minute intervals (9:15 - 15:30 IST)
- open: Option open price
- high: Option high price
- low: Option low price
- close: Option close price (LTP)
- volume: Contracts traded
- open_interest: OI at close
- spot_price: Underlying spot reference
- implied_volatility: IV calculation
- strike_price: Option strike
- option_type: CE (Call) or PE (Put)
- expiry_date: Option expiry
- days_to_expiry: Days remaining

Data Processing:
----------------

7. Simulate trades with real option prices
