📚 Pattern Confirmation – 

Many traders enter a trade as soon as they see a bullish candle, but the strength of the confirmation candle matters much more than its color.

🟥 Weak Confirmation – No Trade
Bullish candle closes below the 50% level of the previous bearish candle.
Buyers are still weak, and sellers may regain control.
Avoid entering early.

🟨 Moderate Confirmation – No Trade
Bullish candle closes above the 50% level of the previous bearish candle.
Buying pressure is improving but not strong enough.
Wait for a stronger confirmation.

🟩 Strong Confirmation – Trade Setup
Bullish candle closes above the opening price of the previous bearish candle.
This indicates strong buying momentum and a higher probability of continuation.
Look for a proper entry only after confirmation and with good risk management.

⚠️ Remember:
A strong candle confirmation increases probability—it does not guarantee a winning trade. Always combine candlestick confirmation with market structure, support & resistance, volume, and proper stop-loss placement.
